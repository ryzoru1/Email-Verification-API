Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

This policy explains what information the Email Verification API (the "Service") processes, why we process it and the choices you have. The Service is operated by [Company name] ("we", "us", "our"). It is offered to developers through RapidAPI, which handles marketplace accounts, API keys and billing.

1. Information we process

  • Email addresses you submit. We process the address, its domain and the verification result in memory to answer your request. We do not build a contact database from submitted addresses.
  • Request metadata. We may log the time, endpoint, response status, latency, IP address, user agent, RapidAPI key identifier and a request ID for security, abuse prevention, capacity planning and debugging.
  • Support messages. If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address and message content.
  • Website data. The marketing site is static and does not set advertising or analytics cookies. Standard server logs may record page requests.

2. How we use information

  • to perform the verification you request and return a result;
  • to operate, secure and troubleshoot the Service and prevent abuse;
  • to understand aggregate usage and plan capacity;
  • to respond to support and legal requests;
  • to comply with applicable law and enforce our Terms of Use.

3. Legal bases

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on performance of our contract with you, our legitimate interests in operating and securing the Service, compliance with legal obligations, and consent where we specifically ask for it. You are responsible for having a lawful basis to submit email addresses for verification.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share limited information with:

  • RapidAPI, to authenticate requests and manage plans, billing and support;
  • Infrastructure providers that host the Service and store operational logs;
  • DNS resolvers and, only when SMTP checking is enabled, the recipient domain's mail servers. In both cases only the domain is disclosed as part of a standard network lookup; message content is never sent;
  • Authorities or advisers when required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety and the Service.

5. Retention

Submitted addresses are processed transiently and are not retained as a contact database. Operational logs are kept for a short period, normally no more than 30 days, unless a longer period is needed for security investigations or legal obligations. Support messages are kept as long as needed to resolve the request and maintain business records.

6. Security

We use HTTPS in transit, access controls, a RapidAPI proxy secret and logging to protect the Service. No system is perfectly secure, so please avoid submitting unnecessary personal data and contact us promptly if you suspect a security issue.

7. International transfers

The Service and its providers may process information in countries other than yours. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for those transfers.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information, or to object to processing and request portability. You can contact us at support@your-domain.example. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

9. Children

The Service is intended for developers and businesses, not children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes
